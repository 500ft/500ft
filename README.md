## Flagship work

### [P-V Fatigue Manifold Proprioception](https://github.com/500ft/P-V-Fatigue-Manifold-Proprioception)

Simulation-only soft-robotics research package and frozen v1.3 manuscript.
Across 2,000 actuator-split traces, P-V loop area tracks fatigue compliance
drift at **r = 0.885** on held-out actuators; the deployed trigger has **no
positive temporal lead**, a limitation reported rather than tuned away.

### [RoboRacer](https://github.com/500ft/RoboRacer)

Vehicle modeling, system identification, controls, and LiDAR-mast structural
validation. The baseline mast **failed** its 200 Hz modal guard at 174.7 Hz;
the redesigned, converged FEA reached **285.5 Hz**. A calibrated physical
compliance test is preregistered and measurement is pending.

### [Self-Stabilizing Drone](https://github.com/500ft/SelfStabilizingDrone)

Guarded micro-UAV release-to-recovery study. The robustness sweep recovers only
**4%** with placeholder torque but predicts 300/300 under an assumed physical
mixer. The deciding 7.0 V bench gate is frozen at **0.020 N·m** plus at least
**962/1,000** primary recovery trials; measurement is pending.
